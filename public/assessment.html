How it works

You will answer two questions out loud, in English:

Question 1 — technical. A work scenario (a bug, an incident, architecture, a trade-off). You will have up to 5 minutes to prepare: think about what to say, make short notes if you like. You can also simply talk about the last feature you built, in technical language.

Question 2 — general. A simple everyday question. No preparation needed.

Each answer is limited to 7 minutes. You will see a reminder when 5 minutes have passed.

Your speech is transcribed automatically — please use Chrome or Edge and allow microphone access. If voice input is not supported on your device, you can type your answer instead.
